The person behind the pixels

Making ideas
playable.

I'm Lillian, a game designer who loves taking an idea and figuring out just how far it can go. Sometimes that means building a platformer around momentum and gravity. Other times, it means creating an entire fantasy world where emotional relationships can literally change reality.\n\nI'm especially interested in the connections between mechanics, storytelling, and worldbuilding: how a small design decision can change the way someone plays, how a rule can shape an entire fictional society, or how an unusual idea can become something surprisingly compelling.\n\nMy projects let me experiment across different creative disciplines, from level design and node-based scripting in Unreal Engine to narrative systems, 3D modeling, and animation in Blender. I'm always looking for something new to learn, a problem worth solving, or an idea that's just a little too interesting to leave alone.
